# CodePop page sources

`codepop.html` introduces CodePop as the site owner's company. Copy is written
in English and summarizes the company material rather than reproducing it.

Sources reviewed on 2026-10-10:

- [Company website](https://www.codepop.tech/en/): product engineering,
  applications, backend platforms, cloud delivery, and production operations.
- [Services](https://www.codepop.tech/en/services): product planning, web and
  mobile applications, APIs, software and cloud architecture, DevOps, platform
  engineering, and DevSecOps. The six cards group these capabilities.
- [Solutions](https://www.codepop.tech/en/solutions): SaaS platforms, customer
  and internal portals, cloud migration, and guarded AI integrations. These are
  service categories, not named client engagements or case studies.
- [Process](https://www.codepop.tech/en/process): discovery and scope, system
  and interface design, incremental development, quality and security checks,
  controlled deployment, and operating documentation. The four stages summarize
  the company's eight-phase process.
- The owner explicitly identifies CodePop as his company. His supplied LinkedIn
  screenshot and `_data/cv.yml` support Founder / CEO since May 2015. The CV
  also supplies the personal contact address used on the page. No separate
  company email, headcount, client names, certifications, or performance claims
  are inferred. The date describes the owner's CodePop work, not legal registration.

Direct requests to the company website returned HTTP 403 in this environment.
The content above was available in indexed copies of the official English pages.
The page links directly to those sources. Ordinary builds use local files and
never fetch company content or assets.

Personal open-source projects remain described as the founder's work. The
illustration reuses the site's existing code artwork; it is not a company logo.
