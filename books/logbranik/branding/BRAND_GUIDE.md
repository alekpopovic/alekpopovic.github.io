# LogBranik brand guide

LogBranik is a proposed brand for the learning project, companion lab and book family. "Log" names the evidence; "branik" is a Serbian word associated with a protective barrier. The name connects the author's working language to an internationally readable technical label. Say it as "log-BRAH-nik". This explanation is brand positioning, not trademark clearance.

The promise is **From logs to controlled action**. We explain observed traffic, bounded decisions, and recoverable operations. We do not promise instant immunity, guaranteed attack prevention, artificial intelligence detection, or production performance that has not been measured.

## Identity hierarchy

- Project brand: **LogBranik**.
- Book title: **Nginx Log Security**.
- Subtitle: **Build a local IP guard, signed policies, and a reliable analysis pipeline**.
- Technical descriptor: **A practical field guide for Nginx Open Source**.
- Implementation label: **LogBranik teaching lab**; edition 0.2.

Keep the book title larger than the project brand on a cover. Keep Nginx in plain text rather than imitating its official logo or implying affiliation. The cover credits **Aleksandar Popovic** (https://github.com/alekpopovic). Keep this author credit legible when exporting a cover.

## Visual concept

Three observation lines converge into a shield. The mark communicates a bounded decision derived from several observations. A check stroke is a local policy outcome, not an assurance seal. Use the mark beside the wordmark when space allows. Use the symbol for small avatars; do not compress the full wordmark into a favicon.

## Colors

| Token | Value | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Navy | `#101D33` | Cover, dark surfaces, headings |
| Teal | `#36D6C2` | Signal lines, accents on dark backgrounds |
| Amber | `#F4B860` | Decisions and intervention emphasis |
| Ink | `#243247` | Body text on light backgrounds |
| Paper | `#F5F7FA` | Light surfaces and text on navy |

Teal and amber are accents. Use ink or navy for long text on paper. Never rely on color alone to distinguish allow and block; add a label or icon. Preserve contrast in exported slides, PDFs, and promotional materials.

## Type and spacing

The delivered assets use DejaVu Sans for headings, DejaVu Serif for reading text, and DejaVu Sans Mono for code. These fonts are available on many Linux systems and support reproducible exports. The PDF embeds the fonts used in its text. A font notice is included with the package.

Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least the height of the check stroke. Prefer the dark wordmark on light backgrounds or the light wordmark on navy. Do not recolor the shield to resemble another vendor's mark, stretch it, add a fake certification badge, or reduce its strokes below legibility.

## Voice

Use precise statements: "The agent applied revision 42"; "The ban expires at its original deadline"; "This deployment gate has not been run." Explain what evidence supports a conclusion. Avoid "unbreakable", "military-grade", "zero latency", "AI powered protection", and fabricated success rates.

The homepage and sales copy should tell a reader what they will build and what knowledge is expected. Implementation detail belongs in technical sections unless it helps a purchase decision. Be candid about the Python/SQLite lab and the separate Go/PostgreSQL production path.

## Files

`logo-light.svg` is a transparent wordmark intended for light surfaces. `logo-dark.svg` includes a navy surface for direct use. `symbol.svg` and `symbol-monochrome.svg` are scalable marks. `book-cover.png` is a 1600 by 2560 typographic cover. `architecture.png` is a factual diagram used in the book. `tokens.json` supplies exact colors and font choices.

This is a reviewable candidate identity. Web searches do not establish legal availability, domain availability, or registration rights. Confirm the final name before investing in domains, filing marks, or presenting the brand as an established company.
