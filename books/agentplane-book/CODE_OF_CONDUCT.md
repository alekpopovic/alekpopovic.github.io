# Community Code of Conduct

Treat contributors respectfully. Critique technical ideas and evidence, not
people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing and disclosure of
private credentials or customer information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions and
restrict participation proportionately. Enforcement should be documented without
exposing private reports. When publishing this repository, maintainers must add a
real private reporting route in SECURITY.md and the repository settings. This
edition does not invent a contact address.

Disagreement is welcome when it helps clarify assumptions, evidence or design
tradeoffs. Provide reproducible examples and avoid personal accusations.
