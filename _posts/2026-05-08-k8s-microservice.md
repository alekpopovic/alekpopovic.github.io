`k8s-microservice` is a Go repository in the Kubernetes and microservice space.

Kubernetes work benefits from small examples that make the service shape explicit: runtime behavior, deployment expectations, health checks, configuration, and container boundaries. This repo sits in that practical learning and scaffolding lane.
