`jaro_winkler` is an Elixir module for calculating Jaro-Winkler distance between two strings.

String similarity appears in matching, cleanup, deduplication, search, and identity resolution. A focused implementation gives Elixir projects a small tool for comparing near-matches without dragging that logic into application code.
