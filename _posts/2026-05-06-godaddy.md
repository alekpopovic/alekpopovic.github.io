`godaddy` is a Ruby repository for GoDaddy-related integration work. Domain and DNS providers often have just enough workflow surface to deserve a small automation layer.

The value of this kind of repo is in repeatability: capture the provider-specific calls once, then let scripts and applications work with a cleaner interface.
