DM Sans and Passion One are distributed under the accompanying SIL Open Font
License files. Original TTFs are from github.com/google/fonts:
  ofl/dmsans/DMSans[opsz,wght].ttf
  ofl/passionone/PassionOne-Regular.ttf
  ofl/passionone/PassionOne-Bold.ttf

DM Sans WOFF2 is a subset for Latin, Latin Extended, Cyrillic, punctuation and
site arrows. Passion One WOFF2 files are unmodified Latin and Latin Extended
webfonts distributed by fonts.gstatic.com through the Google Fonts CSS API.
Family names are unchanged. Original TTFs also provide deterministic typography
for social cards generated by scripts/build-assets.py.
Source: https://github.com/google/fonts

dm-sans-latin.woff2 is built locally from the same licensed original TTF by
scripts/build-assets.py. It retains variable weights 100–1000 and pins the
optical-size axis to 14, with Latin, punctuation and site-symbol coverage.
The wider dm-sans.woff2 remains available for extended Latin and Cyrillic.
No build fetches font data from a remote service.
